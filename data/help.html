The window

The list at the top shows every partition. The bottom part shows each disk as a bar, with its partitions drawn to size. Blue means a partition, grey means free (unallocated) space. A lock on a disk means your system is running from it, so DiskForge won't change it.

Right-click anything to see what you can do with it. Double-click a mounted drive to open it in your file manager.

How to

Use a USB stick: plug it in, right-click it, Mount, then Open in File Manager. Unmount it before you pull it out.

Format: right-click the partition, Format. Everything on it gets erased.

New partition: right-click grey unallocated space, New Partition. A brand new disk needs a partition table first (right-click the disk on the left, New Partition Table).

Resize: right-click the partition, Resize. It can only grow into free space right after it. exFAT can't be resized, XFS can only grow. Back up anything important first.

Rename: select it and press F2.

Delete: right-click, Delete Partition. The space becomes unallocated.

Wipe a whole disk: right-click the disk on the left, New Partition Table. You have to type the disk name to confirm. Pick GPT unless it's for a really old computer.

Which file system?

  • exFAT: USB sticks and external drives, works on Windows, Mac and Linux (needs exfatprogs)
  • FAT32: old devices and boot sticks, but no files over 4 GB
  • NTFS: drives you also use with Windows
  • ext4: Linux-only drives
  • btrfs: Linux with snapshots, at least 256 MB
  • XFS: Linux, big files, can't shrink (needs xfsprogs)

Problems

Something is greyed out: hover over it in the Action menu to see why.

"Not authorized": the password prompt was cancelled, or there's no polkit agent running.

"Device or resource busy": something still has files open on the drive. Close it and try again.

A drive is missing: press F5. Empty card readers are hidden.

Still stuck? Open an issue at github.com/DannyS124/diskforge/issues and paste the output of diskforge --dump.

Shortcuts

F1 help, F2 rename, F5 refresh, Delete delete partition, Alt+Enter properties, Ctrl+Q quit.
